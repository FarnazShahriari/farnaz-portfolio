# Where the About page's flags and logos come from

The files used by the site are in `site/public/about/`, rasterised to PNG.

| File | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flags/no.png`, `flags/gb.png`, `flags/it.png` | [lipis/flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), `flags/1x1/` | MIT |
| `flags/ir-lion-and-sun.png` | [b1690/Iran-flag](https://github.com/b1690/Iran-flag), `Iran-flag.svg` (copy here: `flag-iran-lion-and-sun.svg`), cropped square around the emblem | Repo under GPL-3.0; the flag design itself is public domain |
| `logos/polimi.png` | The seal from the Politecnico di Milano logo in [VictuarVi/PoliMi-PhD-Thesis](https://github.com/VictuarVi/PoliMi-PhD-Thesis), `src/img/logo/blue.svg` | Logo is Politecnico di Milano's trademark |
| `logos/university-of-tehran.png` | [sinamomken/tehran-thesis](https://github.com/sinamomken/tehran-thesis), `img/logo.png` | Logo is University of Tehran's trademark |

Netpower, Piratech and Spentys could not be fetched: their sites, and
Wikimedia, are blocked from the environment the site was built in.
