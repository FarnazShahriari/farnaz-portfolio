# Projects first, no track record, plainer nav

Date: 2026-10-26
Status: confirmed. Supersedes the block order and the track record strip in
`2026-09-17-homepage-information-architecture.md`.

## Decision

- The homepage runs: nav, intro, hero project, more projects, how I work,
  footer. Projects move back before skills.
- The track record strip is removed.
- The nav reads Projects, How I work, About me, Contact. The site name in
  the nav is "Farnaz S. Norheim"; the full name, Farnaz Shahriari Norheim,
  is in the page title and description for search.

## Why

- Recruiters scan for proof first. With projects right after the intro, the
  hero case study is the second thing they see, not the fourth. This is what
  the 2026-09-17 decision argued, before skills were moved up for a while.
- The track record strip had no confirmed names to show, and the projects
  already name the clients. A strip of placeholders added length, not proof.
- "Projects", "How I work" and "About me" say what is behind each link in
  plain words, and match the section titles they lead to.

## Alternatives considered

- **Skills before projects** (the order until now): introduces the method
  before the proof, and pushes the hero project further down.
- **Keep the track record strip with logos:** waits on names and
  permissions, and repeats the clients the projects already show.
